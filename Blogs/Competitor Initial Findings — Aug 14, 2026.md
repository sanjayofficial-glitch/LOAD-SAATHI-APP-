
# Competitor Initial Findings — Aug 14, 2026

## Confirmed business positioning from official pages

### Porter (porter.in)
- On-demand goods transport, primarily intra-city; trucks up to 2,500 kg, two-wheelers, packers and movers.
- Enterprise service for bulk transportation/distribution/supply chain.
- Driver Partner acquisition page.
- City/service landing pages, e.g. /trucks/ahmedabad and similar city pages.
- Official homepage states 15 lakh+ driver partners, 1 crore+ customers, 10 crore+ trips; should be treated as self-reported marketing claims.
- FAQ content covers Porter App, how to use it, charges, prohibited goods, time, city availability and API integration.

### BlackBuck (blackbuck.com)
- Official page is sparse; positioning is India's largest digital trucking platform.
- Search evidence and app pages indicate focus on truck booking/load discovery for shippers, transporters, brokers and truck owners, with fleet management and related trucking tools.

### Rivigo (now surfaced via Mahindra Logistics)
- rivigo.com page redirects/serves Mahindra Logistics B2B Express.
- Official page positions B2B express, surface express, regional distribution, FTL, PTL, intercity/intracity operations, tracking and pan-India pin-code coverage.
- Its current search footprint is more parcel/B2B express and enterprise logistics than LoadSaathi's independent-trucker marketplace focus.

### Fourth brand (“Sher Load”)
- Public search for variants “Sher Load,” “Sherload,” and “Shree Load” did not identify a clear, authoritative India freight competitor.
- Do not attribute rankings to this brand until exact spelling/domain is confirmed; possible intended brand may be a different trucking platform.

Important evidence label: these are public SERP observations and visible competitor page themes, not a complete competitor keyword export. A complete keyword/rank list requires competitor-owned Search Console data or a third-party SEO dataset/API.

## LoadSaathi business fit
- Primary audiences: independent truck drivers/owner-operators and MSME/SME shippers.
- Core service intent: return loads/backhauls, PTL/LTL/FTL freight, truck/load matching, East India route transport, live tracking, rate calculation, faster settlement.
- Avoid generic parcel tracking, courier tracking, and city services not actually offered; these may generate views but poor conversions and can dilute relevance.

## Strategic implication
LoadSaathi should not copy Porter's metro mini-truck/house-shifting SEO or Rivigo's parcel tracking footprint. It should compete for high-intent terms built around independent trucker loads, East India corridors, PTL by state/city, and MSME freight rates, while using route/location pages and tools as conversion pages.

- Mahindra Logistics B2B Express appeared first, with visible sitelink terms `PTL` and `FTL` and a title around B2B Express Services / courier and parcel deliveries.

For LoadSaathi, use the relevant terms `B2B freight transport`, `PTL transport`, `FTL transport`, `freight tracking for MSMEs`, and `truck load tracking`; exclude `Rivigo tracking`, `Rivigo courier tracking`, and generic parcel-tracking queries unless LoadSaathi offers an actual parcel-tracking product.

## BlackBuck public SERP checks

Queries: `BlackBuck truck booking app India`, `find loads for trucks India`, and `BlackBuck truck load app India`.
- `BlackBuck truck booking app India`: visible first-page results included the BlackBuck Google Play app, BlackBuck homepage, BlackBuck Fleet, and App Store listing. Search suggestions included `black buck app download`, `blackbuck app login`, `blackbuck truck app`, `blackbuck truck pan app`, and `blackbuck caller id`.

- `BlackBuck truck load app India`: the result page surfaced Google Play/BlackBuck app content with explicit feature language: `load booking`, `find and book full truck loads`, `caller ID` for transporter details, verified truck-owner/community connection, GPS/route optimization, FASTag and diesel cashback. BlackBuck homepage and Fleet pages were also visible. Related suggestions included `blackbuck app sign in`, `blackbuck app for PC`, `blackbuck app for iPhone`, `blackbuck truck pan app`, `blackbuck trucking app`, `blackbuck caller id`, and `blackbuck truck pan`.

Verified BlackBuck query/page themes: `BlackBuck truck booking app`, `BlackBuck truck load app`, `find loads for trucks`, `full truck load booking`, `truck load app`, `fleet management`, `track your truck and driver`, `transporter caller ID`, `verified truck owners`, `GPS route optimization`, and trucker benefits such as FASTag/diesel cashback. Only the branded/app SERP presence is treated as verified; no exact generic top-10 rank is claimed without an auditable rank dataset.
