## 2026 Estimated Freight Rates: Jamshedpur to Bhubaneswar

Stop overpaying for empty truck space. Below are the estimated 2026 freight rates for the Jamshedpur to Bhubaneswar route. *Note: Rates fluctuate based on fuel prices, seasonality, and live truck availability. Use our [Freight Fare Calculator](/fare-calculator) for an exact, real-time quote.*

### Part Truckload (PTL) Rates
Ideal for MSMEs and smaller shipments. Share the truck space and pay only for the volume or weight your cargo occupies.
*   **Estimated PTL Rate:** ₹4.50 – ₹7.00 per kg (depending on cargo density and handling requirements).

### Full Truckload (FTL) Rates
Best for large, dedicated shipments requiring direct transit without stops.
*   **14ft / 17ft Eicher (approx. 4-5 Tons):** ₹14,000 – ₹18,000
*   **19ft / 22ft Container (approx. 7-9 Tons):** ₹18,000 – ₹24,000
*   **32ft Multi-Axle (approx. 14-21 Tons):** ₹28,000 – ₹36,000
