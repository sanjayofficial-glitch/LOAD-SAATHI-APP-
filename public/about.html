Building the operating system for Indian freight.

LoadSaathi was born from a simple observation: India doesn't have a truck shortage. India has a utilization problem. We're here to fix it.

Founded in Rourkela, Odisha

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To eliminate empty kilometers from Indian logistics by building an AI-powered network that connects every load to its perfect truck — instantly, transparently, and efficiently.

Our Vision

Building India's most efficient freight network — where every kilometer carries value and empty trucks become a thing of the past.

The Problem We Solve

  • Empty trucks return 600+ km on average, wasting ₹10,000–₹25,000 in fuel per round trip.
  • Manual processes and phone-based bookings limit truckers to 15-20 loads per month.
  • No real-time tracking or visibility for shippers moving goods across India.
  • Payment delays, disputes, and manual invoicing add 15-20 days overhead to every transaction.
  • Over 80% of freight brokers charge hidden margins that inflate costs for shippers.
