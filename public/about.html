About LoadSaathi — India's AI Freight Operating System

LoadSaathi is an AI-powered logistics platform founded by Sanjaya Sahu from Rourkela, Odisha. Our mission is to eliminate empty kilometers and build the most efficient freight network in India. With 1 in 3 trucks returning empty and 67% of India's logistics still unorganized, we leverage AI to create a transparent, efficient, and trusted marketplace where every kilometer carries value. Indian logistics market: $350B+ (IBEF 2026).

Our Mission

To eliminate empty kilometers from Indian logistics by building an AI-powered network that connects every load to its perfect truck — instantly, transparently, and efficiently. 40% of truck kilometers in India are run empty, wasting billions in fuel and millions of tons of CO2 annually.

Our Vision

A future where no truck runs empty, every shipment finds its match in seconds, and India's logistics industry operates at peak efficiency — powered by intelligence, not guesswork. Building India's most efficient freight network — where every kilometer carries value and empty trucks become a thing of the past.

The Problem We Solve

  • Empty trucks return 600+ km on average, wasting ₹10,000–₹25,000 in fuel per trip.
  • Manual processes and phone-based bookings limit truckers to 15-20 loads per month.
  • No real-time tracking or visibility for shippers, forcing phone-call updates.
  • Payment delays, disputes, and manual invoicing add 15-20 days overhead.
  • Over 80% of freight brokers charge hidden margins of 10-20%.
  • Fragmented broker networks with opaque pricing erode margins for both shippers and truckers.
  • No standardized credit or trust system forces cash-only transactions.

Our Story

Founded by Sanjaya Sahu, an FTBI alumnus from NIT Rourkela. After years in the Indian logistics industry seeing truckers return empty and shippers struggling to find reliable carriers, he realized the problem wasn't trucks — it was disconnection. LoadSaathi was born to bring transparency, trust, and intelligence to Indian freight. We are not just digitizing existing processes — we are reimagining how logistics works in a connected India.

Our Values

  • Purpose-Driven Innovation: Every feature targets India's 40% empty kilometer problem.

  • Empathy for Both Sides: We design for shippers AND truckers equally.
  • Speed & Simplicity: Obsess over reducing friction from registration to payment.
