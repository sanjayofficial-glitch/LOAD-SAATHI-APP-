# Vulnerability reporting for LoadSaathi

We take security reports seriously and aim to acknowledge every valid report
within 2 business days.

## What to report

- Authentication bypass, session handling or authorization flaws
- Cross-site scripting (XSS), CSRF or content injection
- SQL injection, insecure direct object reference, or data exposure between
  shipper, trucker and admin accounts
- Payment, escrow or credit-score manipulation
- GPS/location spoofing that materially affects trip integrity
- Denial of service against the API or Realtime layer

## What not to report

- Missing security headers with no demonstrated impact
- Rate-limit or scraping findings against public marketing pages
- Findings from automated scanners without a working proof of concept
- Social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks
- Bugs that require an already-compromised account to reach

## How to report

Email **security@loadsaathi.in** with:

1. Type of issue and affected area (for example "shipper dashboard")
2. Steps to reproduce, ideally minimal
3. Impact and any account IDs or request IDs involved
4. Whether the issue is already public

Please encrypt sensitive reports. Our PGP public key is available on request.

You will receive an acknowledgement within 2 business days and a status update
at least every 7 days until the issue is resolved.

Do not access other users' data, run automated scans against production, or
disrupt service while testing. We will not pursue legal action for good-faith
research that stays within these bounds.

## Disclosure

We do not offer bug bounties at this time. Reporters may be credited in our
release notes with their permission, or kept anonymous on request. We will give
a target remediation window of 90 days for medium severity issues and 30 days for
high or critical severity issues once a fix is confirmed.
