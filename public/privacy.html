Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At LoadSaathi, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform — India's AI-powered shared freight marketplace connecting shippers and truckers for PTL and LTL loads across East India. Please read this policy carefully. By using LoadSaathi, you consent to the data practices described. LoadSaathi is operated by LoadSaathi Technologies Pvt. Ltd., First Floor, FTBI, T1-109, National Institute of Technology, Sector 1, Rourkela, Odisha 769008, India.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register for LoadSaathi, we collect information that identifies you as an individual. This includes your full name, email address, phone number, physical address, government-issued identification numbers (such as Aadhaar, PAN, or Driver's License), and business registration details. We also collect profile data such as company name, vehicle type for truckers, and verification documents.

Transaction Information

We collect details about every transaction on our platform including shipment origins and destinations, cargo descriptions, weight and budget, pricing information, payment records, GPS tracking data, route information via OSRM, and communication logs between users via our messaging system. This powers our AI matching and credit scoring.

Device & Usage Information

We automatically collect information about how you interact with our platform including your IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, pages visited, time spent, referral URLs, and interactions with our AI features. We use PostHog, Google Analytics, and Vercel Analytics with consent.

How We Use Your Information

Providing Platform Services

We use your information to facilitate matches between shippers and transporters, process payments via escrow, enable real-time GPS tracking with Leaflet, and provide customer support. Your data enables our 7-factor matching algorithm: city match, proximity, route overlap, capacity fit, price alignment, date match, and rating bonus.

Trust & Safety

Your data powers our Digital Freight Credit Score system (300-900) based on completion rate 30%, on-time delivery 25%, communication quality 15%, review ratings 20%, and account tenure 10%. It enables identity verification, detects fraudulent activity, and maintains our bidirectional review system.

Improving Our Platform

We analyze usage patterns to improve our AI matching algorithms, enhance user experience, develop new features like price prediction via Gemini, and optimize platform performance. Aggregated statistics are cited with sources, e.g., Indian logistics market: $350B+ (IBEF 2026).

Communications

We send transactional notifications about your shipments, payments, and account activity via Supabase Realtime and push notifications. With your consent, we may also send marketing communications about platform updates and new features. You can opt out at any time.

Information Sharing

With Other Users

When you engage in a transaction, certain information is shared with the counterparty — such as your name, contact details, and credit score. This is necessary to facilitate logistics operations and build trust.

Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party service providers for Clerk authentication, Supabase database, payment processing, identity verification, cloud hosting on Vercel, and analytics (PostHog, Google Analytics). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and are listed in our subprocessors.

Legal Compliance

We may disclose your information when required by law, court order, or governmental regulation in India. We will notify you of such requests when legally permitted. Data retention complies with Indian IT Act and GST e-way bill rules.

No Data Selling

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve LoadSaathi services. We do not share data with brokers for unrelated marketing.

Data Security

Encryption

All data transmitted through LoadSaathi is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 protocols. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption in Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS).

Access Controls

We implement strict access controls and authentication mechanisms via Clerk JWT to ensure that only authorized personnel can access user data. All access is logged and audited. Supabase RLS policies enforce per-user row access.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services and comply with legal retention (e.g., 7 years for financial records). You may request deletion via privacy@loadsaathi.com.

Your Rights

Access & Portability

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and to receive it in a structured, commonly used format (JSON/CSV). Contact privacy@loadsaathi.com.

Correction & Deletion

You may update or correct your information through your account settings at /profile. You can also request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements.

Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on your consent to process your data (e.g., analytics cookies via CookieHub), you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time via cookie settings. This does not affect prior processing.

Grievance Officer

Under India's IT Rules, our Grievance Officer is at privacy@loadsaathi.com, LoadSaathi Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. We respond within 30 days.
