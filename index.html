LoadSaathi - India's AI-Powered Freight Marketplace

LoadSaathi connects shippers and truckers for PTL (Partial Truckload) and LTL (Less Than Truckload) loads across East India. The platform uses AI matching, real-time GPS tracking, digital credit scores, and escrow-backed payments to eliminate empty return trips and reduce freight costs by up to 40%. Shippers in Odisha, Jharkhand, and West Bengal post loads on the Rourkela-Ranchi-Burdwan corridor and beyond; truckers and fleet owners find return loads, fill empty capacity, and build a digital reputation with a 300-900 credit score.

Who It's For

  • Shippers: SMEs and enterprises in Odisha, Jharkhand, and West Bengal who need to move goods on the Rourkela-Ranchi-Burdwan corridor and beyond.
  • Truckers & Fleet Owners: Independent truckers and fleet operators looking to fill empty capacity, find return loads, and build a digital reputation.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Matching: Multi-factor scoring engine that matches shippers with truckers in seconds.
  • Real-Time GPS Tracking: Live location sharing with ETAs, route visualization, and geofence alerts.
  • Digital Freight Credit Score: 300-900 score for every participant based on completion rates and reviews.
  • AI Price Prediction: Market-aligned pricing suggestions based on route, demand, and historical data.
  • Escrow Payments: Secure milestone-based payment releases with dispute resolution.
  • Bidirectional Reviews: Both shippers and truckers rate each other after every completed trip.

Pricing

Free to join. Transaction fee: 2-5% per completed shipment. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no minimum commitments. Enterprise pricing for 50+ loads per month.

Cities Served

Rourkela, Ranchi, Bhubaneswar, Kolkata, Jamshedpur, and 50+ cities across Odisha, Jharkhand, and West Bengal. 25+ active freight corridors including Rourkela-Ranchi-Burdwan, Rourkela-Kolkata, and Bhubaneswar-Kolkata. Indian logistics market: $350B+ (IBEF 2026).
