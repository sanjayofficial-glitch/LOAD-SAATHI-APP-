## Asset Details

### 1. Freight Rate Methodology (P0)

**Purpose:** Explain how LoadSaathi calculates freight rates, building trust and enabling citation.

**Required Data:**
- Rate calculation formula
- Data sources (diesel prices, tolls, demand signals)
- How AI price prediction works
- How return-load pricing differs
- Update frequency

**Sources to Verify:**
- Internal pricing model documentation
- Historical rate data accuracy
- Comparison with market benchmarks

**CTA:** "Try the calculator" → /tools/fare-calculator

**Internal Links:** /tools/fare-calculator, /tools/freight-rate-calculator, /pricing

**Reviewer:** Operations

---

### 2. Route Coverage Methodology (P0)

**Purpose:** Explain how LoadSaathi determines which routes to publish, preventing doorway-page concerns.

**Required Data:**
- Criteria for adding a new route
- How distances are verified (OSRM)
- How transit times are estimated
- How often routes are reviewed
- Current coverage map

**Sources to Verify:**
- OSRM routing data
- Historical shipment data
- Operations team knowledge

**CTA:** "View all routes" → /routes

**Internal Links:** /routes, /location/*

**Reviewer:** Operations

---

### 3. Freight Glossary (P1)

**Purpose:** Define industry terms (PTL, LTL, FTL, e-way bill, LR, POD, etc.) for citation by AI systems.

**Required Data:**
- List of terms to define
- Accurate definitions
- Hindi translations where relevant
- Related terms and cross-references

**CTA:** "Browse glossary" → /glossary

**Internal Links:** /blog/*, /guide/*

**Reviewer:** Content team

---

### 4. Shipment Case Study Template (P1)

**Purpose:** Template for publishing real customer success stories with verifiable data.

**Template Fields:**
- Customer name (with permission)
- Industry and location
- Shipment details (route, weight, cargo type)
- Challenge faced
- Solution provided
- Results (with specific metrics)
- Quote from customer (with permission)

**CTA:** "Share your story" → /contact

**Internal Links:** /safety-trust, /pricing

**Reviewer:** Operations + Legal

---

### 5. Odisha Freight Insights (P1)

**Purpose:** Original research report on Odisha's freight ecosystem, establishing LoadSaathi as a regional expert.

**Required Data:**
- Odisha freight volume and growth
- Key industries and commodities
- Infrastructure developments
- Corridor analysis
- Seasonal patterns

**CTA:** "Explore Odisha routes" → /routes

**Internal Links:** /routes, /location/rourkela, /location/bhubaneswar

**Reviewer:** Operations

## Content Evidence Requirements

Each asset must pass the Content Evidence Checklist before publishing:

1. All statistical claims have sources
2. All rate figures are labeled "indicative" with date
3. All coverage claims are verified
4. Author is verified real person
5. Reviewer has approved
6. Methodology is documented
7. Limitations are stated
8. No prohibited language (best, cheapest, guaranteed)
