One-line story Trucks run empty on return legs; shippers overpay brokers 10–20% and can't track goods. LoadSaathi matches them with AI, tracks live, holds money in escrow, and scores trust — taking ~2–3% per deal.
2 · Users

Two users, two goals

Shipper (blue)

  • Goal: move goods cheaply, reliably, visibly
  • Posts: shipment — route, weight, goods, budget
  • Watches: spending, live truck map, upcoming loads
  • Pays: 1.5–3% of shipment value per deal

Trucker (orange)

  • Goal: fill every kilometer, earn more, no empty returns
  • Posts: trip — route, capacity, price, vehicle, date
  • Watches: earnings, credit gauge, nearby loads
  • Pays: 1.5–2.5% of trip value per deal

No blind reviews (retaliation risk). Three different tier-name sets for the same score. No achievements engine behind "Gold badge" copy.
7 · Tracking

Live GPS: who sees what

Shipper sees

  • "Trucks Around You" live map
  • LIVE (green) vs POSTED TRIPS (orange) badge
  • Filters: All / In Transit / Standing
  • Tap truck → route, price, name, rating, verified

Trucker sees

  • "Loads within X km" (50 / 75 / 100 km)
  • Nearest-first list + blue "you are here" dot
  • Start/Stop tracking card: accuracy, speed, heading
  • One-tap trip start
Know the limitsRoute = distance + time + orange line. No tolls, no live traffic, no auto ETA. "50 km arrival alert" is marketing copy — no such job exists yet. Web tracking stops when the tab closes (true background needs the Android app).
Bonus: emissions cardShows kg CO₂e per route (+ per tonne-km). Free to compute, works offline — an enterprise/ESG wedge. Future: monthly sustainability reports for shippers.
